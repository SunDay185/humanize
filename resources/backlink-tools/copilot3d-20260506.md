# Copilot 3D

Copilot 3D is a lightweight 3D creation workflow for product visualization, early concept exploration, and fast mockup iteration.

- Official site: [Copilot 3D](https://copilot3d.net/)
- Category: AI 3D creation
- Notes: Useful for turning early product or accessory ideas into 3D concept drafts before full CAD or rendering work.
